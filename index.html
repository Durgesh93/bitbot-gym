What happened

Starting MakeCode… It opens with the 4tronix Bit:Bot extension already added, so both sets of drawers are there. This part needs the internet.

Build your blocks on the left and press Run. Every time MakeCode saves, the gym takes the code, so Run always plays what you can see. Or press Open a .hex to load a program you have already built.

The room 0.0 s

Shadow Monster learns how bright the room is when you press Run, then sleeps. Press Run, wait for the sleepy eyes, then switch the torch on — that is the jump it is waiting for. Drag the torch to chase it into the corners - it runs away from light, it does not follow it.
